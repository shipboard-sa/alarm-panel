Wiring Notes for Pushbuttons

Each button has 4 terminals:
  • NO + COM → wire to H4 or H5 2-pin header on PCB (switch contacts)
  • LED+ → panel +24V rail (tap from any connector's +24V pin)
  • LED− → panel GND rail

MUTE button switch → H5 header. TEST button switch → H4 header.
